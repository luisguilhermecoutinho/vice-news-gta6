63 official GTA 6 screenshots — we analyzed everything Rockstar revealed

Jun 24, 2026 · By Vice News · Source: Rockstar Games

On June 24, 2026, Rockstar Games did what the community had been waiting months for: released 63 official screenshots of GTA 6 and revealed a series of gameplay details that changed how we understand the game. This wasn't a simple image dump — each screenshot was chosen to communicate something about the world of Leonida.

Classic Car Collection

The biggest gameplay reveal from the 63 screenshots was the confirmation of the Classic Car Collection, an Ultimate Edition exclusive mechanic. Multiple screenshots show classic vehicles in states of abandonment scattered across the map, as well as a garage interface for restoring them. Rockstar confirmed that each car has its own story within the game's universe.

Real-time protagonist swap

Multiple screenshots show the swap system between Jason and Lucia during missions — including a high-speed escape sequence where both are in separate vehicles. The swap appears to work with a selection wheel similar to GTA V's system, but with more context information (health, position, and wanted level for each character).

🎮 Key detail: Unlike GTA V where the swap was essentially cosmetic between missions, in GTA 6 swapping during missions appears to have direct tactical impact — switching to Lucia in a foot chase vs. Jason in a vehicle could be the difference between escaping or not.

Dynamic world events

Multiple screenshots show situations that are clearly not scripted missions: building fires with firefighters responding, traffic accidents, street protests, and even an approaching hurricane over Leonida's coast. Rockstar confirmed that Leonida has a dynamic events system that occurs independently of the player's actions.

Expanded customization

For the first time in the series, Jason and Lucia have extensive appearance customization systems — clothing, accessories, tattoos, hairstyles — with hundreds of confirmed options. Screenshots show clothing stores inside enterable shopping malls and retail centers.

In-game social media

GTA 6 has a fictional in-game social media system — "Lifeinvader" and "Bleeter" evolved into a more complete experience. NPCs post content, react to world events, and you can interact with the platform from the in-game phone. Rockstar showed screenshots of character profiles with followers, posts, and reactions.

  • 63 screenshots released on June 24
  • 54+ vehicles identified by the community
  • 700+ locations enterable confirmed
  • In-game social media with follower system
  • Dynamic events independent of missions

What the community discovered in the screenshots

The GTA community took less than 24 hours to dissect all 63 images. Some notable discoveries: at least three distinct biomes are visible — Miami-style urban coast, Everglades-reminiscent swamp areas, and rural areas with dense vegetation. One screenshot shows what appears to be a large-scale international airport, confirming air travel as part of the map. Another shows a marina packed with yachts and boats, with a watercraft customization system visible in the UI.

Graphics and console generation

The graphical leap over GTA V is brutal and evident in every screenshot. Lighting quality — especially at dusk and night — is on another level: real-time reflections on wet surfaces, volumetric lighting in interiors, and shadows at a resolution far superior to what previous consoles allowed. GTA 6 was built from the ground up for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, and the screenshots confirm Rockstar made the most of this generation's hardware.
