Leonida Map: Complete Guide to GTA 6's 6 Regions

By Vice News · Updated July 2026 · ~12 min read · Based on official trailers and Rockstar's 63 screenshots

The state of Leonida in GTA 6 is an unprecedented feat of open-world engineering. Inspired by real Florida — with Vice City mirroring Miami — Leonida isn't just bigger than any previous map in the series. It's, for the first time, a state that feels like a real state: with geographic, cultural and economic diversity that goes far beyond previous cities in the series.

In this article, based on Rockstar's two official trailers, 63 screenshots, and extensive community analysis (particularly the State of Leonida project), we detail each of the 6 confirmed regions — what makes them unique, what you can do in each, and why every zone deserves your time and attention.

Sources: Trailer 1 (December 2023), Trailer 2 (May 2025), 63 official Rockstar screenshots (June 2026), State of Leonida community project analysis, Take-Two investor call statements.

The 6 Regions of Leonida

  1. Vice City — The Neon Metropolis
  2. Leonida Keys — The Southern Islands
  3. Grassrivers — The Swamps
  4. Port Gellhorn — The Industrial Port
  5. Hamlet — The Suburbs
  6. Kelly County — The Open Country

Leonida Map by the Numbers

What has been confirmed about Leonida's scale:

🗺️ 6 confirmed regions 🏢 700+ enterable locations 🚇 Vice City metro 🌊 Extensive coastline and maritime zone 🏙️ Vice City (Miami-inspired) 🌿 Swamp area (Everglades-inspired)

Rockstar Games confirmed over 700 enterable locations — a number that surpasses any previous game in the series by several multiples. These include nightclubs, motels, restaurants, pawn shops, supermarkets, fast food joints, gun stores, art galleries, banks, hospitals, and much more.

1. Vice City — The Neon Metropolis

🌆 Vice City

Inspired by Miami Beach, South Beach, Downtown Miami, and Coral Gables

Vice City is the heart of GTA 6 — and arguably the most impressive urban environment Rockstar has ever created. The two trailers gave us images of Ocean Drive at sunset, pink neons reflecting on the water, helikiteboarding in the bay, luxury car chases down palm-lined avenues, and the frantic nightlife that made the real Miami world-famous.

What sets it apart from Los Santos in GTA V is density and cultural authenticity. Vice City is a city with its own soul — music, art, ethnic diversity, the brutal contrast between extreme wealth and visible poverty, and a per-square-meter excess ratio unmatched in the series.

Confirmed neighborhoods in Vice City

  • Ocean Drive / South Beach: The most iconic area — Art Deco, neons, luxury hotels, beaches with GTA VI's signature colors. Lucia is strongly associated with this area in the trailers.
  • Downtown Vice City: The financial center, with skyscrapers, banks, and the kind of high-tension urban environment perfect for large-scale heists.
  • Little Haiti / Immigrant Neighborhoods: Visible in the trailers, with murals, street markets, and the cultural vibe that makes Vice City unique in the series.
  • Cayo Leonida / Port Areas: The waterfront zone near the city port, with contrasts between maritime luxury and hard labor.
  • Luxury Residences: Mansions on artificial islands and gated communities with private security — obvious targets for high-class heist missions.
🌃 Vice City tip: learn Ocean Drive's layout in the first few days. It's probably where you'll spend the most time on early missions, and knowing every alley and shortcut will save you from many complicated chases.

2. Leonida Keys — The Southern Islands

🌴 Leonida Keys

Inspired by the Florida Keys and Biscayne Bay islands

The Leonida Keys are the state's southern extension — a chain of islands connected by bridges, with a radically different character from Vice City. Where Vice City is frantic and urban, the Keys are slower-paced, with fishing communities, luxury marinas, resorts, and the kind of quiet that hides the worst secrets.

In the trailers we see Jason and Lucia in the Keys in very different contexts: Lucia in high-tension situations near private port facilities, Jason in scenarios suggesting contacts with smuggling networks operating between the islands. Water is everywhere here — those who can't swim and pilot boats will be at a disadvantage.

What to do in the Leonida Keys

  • Underwater exploration: With miles of coastline and crystal-clear water, the Keys will certainly have treasures, relics, and underwater missions. Invest in diving gear early.
  • Maritime activities and boat races: Rockstar confirmed watercraft in the portfolio — the Shitzu Squalo (Ultimate Edition) is just the start. The Keys will be the game's aquatic playground.
  • Smuggling and illegal networks: The island geography is perfect for smuggling operations — discreet entry and exit points, limited surveillance, and wealthy clients at private marinas.
  • Luxury properties: The Keys likely have the most expensive and exclusive residences on the entire map — targets for high-value heists and premium real estate investment opportunities.

3. Grassrivers — The Swamps de Leonida

🌿 Grassrivers

Inspired by the Florida Everglades and the wetlands of the southern US

Grassrivers is probably the most original area Rockstar has ever created in a GTA — a vast, atmospheric swamp that contrasts with everything the series has done before. No neons here, no mansions. Just water, dense vegetation, dangerous wildlife, and a particular tension that comes from isolation.

Jason Duval is clearly tied to this area. The trailers show him in Grassrivers scenarios suggesting deep knowledge of the territory — moving through the swamps at night, interacting with communities that are off the authorities' radar. Grassrivers is the territory of those who don't want to be found.

What makes Grassrivers unique

  • Wildlife: Alligators, snakes, and other Florida swamp fauna. GTA 6 will likely have the most developed animal system in the series — and in Grassrivers, that means genuine environmental danger.
  • Aquatic mobility: Much of Grassrivers is only navigable by boat or airboat. Having a watercraft here isn't optional — it's necessary.
  • Rural smuggling networks: The area is perfect for operations that need to stay off Vice City's radar. Clandestine labs, hidden depots, and escape routes into the state's interior.
  • Unique atmosphere: No previous GTA area looks anything like Grassrivers at night — the moon's reflection on dark water, swamp sounds, and the awareness that you're very far from any hospital.
🐊 Survival warning: get out of your car in Grassrivers with caution. GTA 6's wildlife is a real mechanic, not just environmental decoration. An alligator can significantly shorten your gaming session.

4. Port Gellhorn — The Industrial Port

🏭 Port Gellhorn

Inspired by the Port of Miami and Florida's coastal industrial port complexes

Port Gellhorn is the legal — and illegal — economic engine of Leonida. This is where goods enter and leave the state, and where criminal organizations run their most lucrative import-export operations. In the trailers, we see stacked containers, port cranes, massive warehouses, and the industrial environment that serves as backdrop for heists and smuggling missions.

Police and private security presence in Port Gellhorn is high — but so are the rewards. Large cargo theft operations, warehouse heists, and control of maritime routes are the main business in this zone.

Opportunities in Port Gellhorn

  • Vehicle import-export: Warehouses full of rare cars awaiting shipment — perfect opportunities for the vehicle collection mechanic.
  • Cargo heists: Ships with valuable loads, containers of illegal equipment, and nighttime operations that pay very well.
  • Territory control: In GTA Online, Port Gellhorn will likely be a high-contention zone between organizations — controlling routes here means significant passive income.
  • Maritime access: The port location means direct sea access — boats can arrive and depart discreetly, making Port Gellhorn ideal for operations that need aquatic escape routes.

5. Hamlet — The Suburbs de Leonida

🏘️ Hamlet

Inspired by greater Miami's suburbs and central Florida's residential communities

Hamlet is Leonida's most "normal" area — and for that reason, one of the most interesting. American suburbs have a look of calm that hides tension and secrets, and Rockstar knows better than anyone how to exploit this contrast. Hamlet has shopping centers, schools, parks, row houses, and the suburban life that has never been portrayed in such detail in a GTA game.

Hamlet's density is lower than Vice City's, but the variety of activities and opportunities is different — not better or worse, just another dimension of Leonida. Certain story missions will certainly pass through Hamlet, exploring the contrast between apparently normal life and criminal activities happening beneath the surface.

What to do in Hamlet

  • Stealth missions: Hamlet is the ideal environment for operations requiring discretion — infiltrations, surveillance, and movements that need to stay under the radar.
  • Shopping and services: With shopping centers and a variety of stores, Hamlet is a good place for equipment, upgrades, and everyday character activities.
  • Leisure activities: Parks, golf courses, sports centers — the recreational activities Rockstar always includes as a way to develop character stats.

6. Kelly County — The Open Country

🌾 Kelly County

Inspired by rural inland Florida — Osceola County, Highlands County, and agricultural zones

Kelly County is Leonida's open country — the territory Rockstar built for Jason Duval, representing a side of GTA 6 radically different from anything we've played in the series. Farms, endless back roads, small rural communities with their own hierarchies and tensions, and a relationship with the land and the law very different from Vice City.

Kelly County is large, but enterable location density is lower than in urban areas. What exists here are open spaces ideal for high-speed driving, shooting ranges without immediate police interference, and communities that operate by their own rules.

Kelly County and Jason Duval

Kelly County is clearly Jason's home turf. The trailers show him comfortable in this environment — he knows the people, the roads, the secrets the community keeps. This character familiarity with the territory will certainly translate into gameplay advantages: Jason-exclusive missions in this zone, contacts only he can access, and escape routes that those who don't know the territory would never find.

  • Limitless driving: Deserted back roads and open fields — paradise for high-speed chases without traffic or immediate police.
  • Rural smuggling operations: Leonida's interior is perfect for trafficking networks that need distance from urban authorities.
  • Hunting and outdoor activities: GTA 6 will likely have hunting and nature activities similar to Red Dead Redemption 2 — in Kelly County, these activities make complete narrative sense.

The 700+ Enterable Locations — What This Means

Rockstar confirmed over 700 enterable locations in Leonida, a number that fundamentally changes what "open world" means in a video game. For context: GTA V had a few dozen enterable interiors. In GTA 6, entering buildings isn't an exception — it's the norm.

What we can expect to find among those 700+ locations:

  • Nightclubs and bars — with live music, dynamic crowds, and social interaction opportunities
  • Restaurants and fast food — where you can eat to restore health
  • Pawn shops — to sell stolen items
  • Supermarkets and convenience stores — both to buy from and rob
  • Banks — obvious heist targets with varied security systems
  • Hospitals — where you respawn and can buy medical care
  • Gun stores — with extensive weapon customization
  • Garages and workshops — to store and customize vehicles
  • Hotels and safehouses — to save and recover
  • Entertainment centers — cinemas, arcades, bowling clubs, golf courses
🗺️ Exploration tip: don't try to see everything on day one. Set exploration zones per session — one part of Vice City per session, one visit to a new neighborhood. GTA 6 was designed to be discovered gradually, and the surprise of finding an unexpected location is a fundamental part of the experience.

The Vice City Metro

One of the most exciting confirmations about Vice City is the existence of a fully functional metro system — a first for the GTA series. In the trailers, we see modern metro stations integrated into Vice City's urban landscape, with moving trains and platforms full of NPCs.

The Vice City metro will likely function as fast transport between city zones — but also as a mission setting and alternative escape route. Metro chases, platform robberies, and the dynamic of escaping into underground lines are gameplay opportunities Rockstar will certainly explore.

Comparison with Previous Series Maps

To put Leonida's scale in perspective:

  • GTA III (Liberty City): One city, 3 districts. The start of the 3D era.
  • GTA Vice City: One island, '80s setting. Iconic classic.
  • GTA San Andreas: 3 cities + open country. The biggest map until GTA V.
  • GTA IV (Liberty City): A dense version of New York. Focus on quality.
  • GTA V (Los Santos + Blaine County): The biggest map in the series until now.
  • GTA 6 (Leonida): 6 distinct regions, 700+ interiors, two protagonists, all of Florida as inspiration.

Leonida isn't just the biggest GTA map — it's arguably the most ambitious open world ever created for an action game.

How to Explore Leonida Efficiently

With a map of this size, unplanned exploration can result in wasted hours. Here are strategies for exploring Leonida efficiently:

  1. Start from the center and expand outward. Vice City first, then adjacent zones. Don't try to start in the suburbs or countryside.
  2. Use different vehicles for different zones. A motorcycle for Vice City, an off-road for Kelly County, a boat for the Keys and Grassrivers.
  3. Mark locations on the map as you discover them. The waypoint system will be your best ally on a map of this scale.
  4. Follow the characters to their natural territories. Jason will show you the best of Kelly County and Grassrivers; Lucia will guide you through the best of Vice City.
  5. Accept side missions as tourist guides. Side missions are often the best way to discover new locations and mechanics.

Leonida is a state Rockstar Games spent probably a decade building. It deserves to be discovered with patience and curiosity — each region has its own character, and every location has something to offer. Happy exploring.
