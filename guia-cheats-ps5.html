GTA 6 Cheats PS5 — Complete Cheat Codes List

Updated: November 2026 · By Vice News · vicenewsgta6.com

GTA 6 launches on November 19, 2026 for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Like every GTA game before it, GTA 6 will have a full set of cheat codes you can enter during gameplay to unlock weapons, max out health and armor, change your wanted level, spawn vehicles, and alter the world around you.

How to Enter Cheats on PS5

In GTA 5, cheats were entered either via the in-game phone dial pad or by pressing a specific button combination on the controller. GTA 6 is expected to use a similar system — most likely through the in-game phone, which both Jason and Lucia carry throughout the story.

We'll update this page the moment the game is out with every confirmed cheat code.

📱 Tip: In GTA 5, cheats entered via the phone were more reliable than button combos and worked across all platforms. Expect GTA 6 to work the same way.

What Cheats to Expect

Based on GTA 5 and the GTA series tradition, these are the cheat categories that almost certainly return in GTA 6:

  • Health & Armor — restore to full instantly
  • Wanted Level — clear your stars or max them out
  • Weapons — spawn a full weapon loadout
  • Vehicle Spawns — spawn specific cars, bikes, helicopters, boats
  • World Effects — slow motion, explosive bullets, drunk mode
  • Invincibility — god mode for a limited time

Will Cheats Disable Trophies/Achievements?

In GTA 5, using cheats disabled trophies and achievements for that session — you had to reload your save to re-enable them. The same restriction is expected in GTA 6. If you care about Platinum, don't use cheats.
