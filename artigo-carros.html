The Classic Car Collection (Ultimate Edition)

One of the Ultimate Edition's exclusive mechanics is the Classic Car Collection — a set of abandoned vehicles scattered across the Leonida map that the player can find, recover, and restore. Each car has its own story within the game's universe and allows unique modifications unavailable on other vehicles.

🔍 Important note: The Classic Car Collection is exclusive gameplay content for the Ultimate Edition — not just skins. The cars need to be found and restored. Standard Edition buyers don't have access to this mechanic.

Returning classics

Rockstar confirmed the return of several iconic series vehicles, redesigned with brutal detail for next-gen engines: the Karin Futo (GTA Online's quintessential drift machine) returns with completely rebuilt driving physics. The Declasse Sabre Turbo was caught directly in heist escape footage with Jason and Lucia at the wheel. The Albany Buccaneer Custom brings the lowrider flavor Vice City demands.

Notable new vehicles

Beyond the returning vehicles, GTA 6 introduces completely new designs. The Principe Alvino V1 (confirmed in June) is an Italian naked bike that promises to be the go-to for cruising Vice City at high speed. The Vapid Ganado — Jason's American pickup in the Extended Look — is the epitome of the male protagonist's ride: robust, versatile, and with retro mods available in the Ultimate Edition.

Watercraft and aquatic vehicles

With Leonida having an extensive coastline and the Keys to the south, aquatic vehicles will have far more relevance than in previous games. The Squalo (Ultimate Edition exclusive speedboat) and several other watercraft were confirmed in the official screenshots.
