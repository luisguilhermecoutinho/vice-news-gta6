Editorial process — how we verify information

Information quality is our priority. We follow a rigorous editorial process:

1
Primary sourceAll news starts from official sources (Rockstar Newswire, Take-Two, press releases) or reputable international media outlets.
2
Cross-verificationBefore publishing, we confirm with at least two independent sources. Unconfirmed leaks are always labeled as such.
3
Context and analysisWe don't just report — we add context, compare with previous games, and give our honest analysis.
4
UpdatesIf previous information becomes outdated or incorrect, we update the article and note the changes.
5
TransparencyOpinion articles are clearly distinguished from news. We never publish paid content without identifying it as such.
