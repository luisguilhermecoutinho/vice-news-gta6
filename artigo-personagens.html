GTA 6 marks a historic first for the series: two playable protagonists from the start, both equally important to the story. Lucia and Jason are at the center of a criminal conspiracy in Leonida — two people who were already down on their luck before everything went wrong. Here's everything confirmed about them and the wider cast.

Lucia Caminos

Lucia Caminos
Co-Protagonist

Lucia is the first female playable protagonist in a mainline GTA game. She's introduced serving time at Leonida State Correctional Institute — and the Extended Look opens with her at a parole hearing, trying to convince the board she's ready to re-enter society.

Her background suggests she's been in and out of trouble with the law, likely from circumstances rather than pure choice. She's sharp, resourceful, and carries herself with a quiet confidence that makes her compelling from the first scene.

First female GTA protagonist Playable from the start Florida-based

Jason Duval

Jason Duval
Co-Protagonist

Jason is the male co-protagonist. Where Lucia is seen as calculating and composed, Jason comes across as more impulsive — quicker to act, harder to read. The Extended Look makes clear that their dynamic is central to the story's tension.

His backstory hasn't been fully revealed, but dialogue and scenes from the Extended Look suggest he and Lucia met through criminal circles in Leonida. He's not someone who planned for this life — circumstances have put him here.

Male co-protagonist Playable from the start More impulsive character

Their Relationship

Partners, not just in crime

The official description says Jason and Lucia are "forced to rely on each other more than ever if they want to make it out alive." Their relationship is clearly the emotional core of GTA 6 — whether it's romantic, platonic, or something that evolves is one of the story's central questions.

The Extended Look shows moments of real tension between them — disagreements about how to handle a situation, moments of vulnerability, and moments of genuine trust. Rockstar has clearly invested in making their dynamic feel earned rather than convenient.

"Jason and Lucia have always known the deck is stacked against them. But when an easy score goes wrong, they find themselves on the darkest side of the sunniest place in America." — Official Rockstar description

The "easy score gone wrong" setup is classic heist drama, but the Extended Look suggests the conspiracy they stumble into goes far deeper than a single job. Both characters are in over their heads — and that shared vulnerability is what binds them.

Villains & Antagonists

Rockstar hasn't revealed the main antagonist of GTA 6, which is typical — GTA games rarely show the primary villain in early marketing. However, several scenes in the Extended Look hint at a larger conspiracy involving:

  • Law enforcement corruption — multiple scenes show crooked cops and DEA agents working with or against the protagonists
  • Organized crime — Vice City has multiple criminal factions operating simultaneously across different neighborhoods
  • Political connections — Leonida's criminal landscape appears to extend into local government and business
  • A figure referred to as "the Motherflipper" — a colorful local criminal who appears in comedy-adjacent scenes but may have a larger role

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you switch between Lucia and Jason?

Yes. Like GTA 5 allowed switching between Michael, Trevor and Franklin, GTA 6 will allow players to switch between Lucia and Jason. The Extended Look shows both perspectives. Some missions may require one specific character.

Is Lucia the main protagonist or are they equal?

Rockstar has presented them as equal protagonists. Lucia is introduced first and appears slightly more in the marketing material, but both characters are central to the story with no indication of one being "more main" than the other.

Are Lucia and Jason a couple?

Rockstar hasn't confirmed a romantic relationship, but the Extended Look shows strong chemistry and mutual reliance. The story description emphasizes they must "rely on each other more than ever" — whether that develops romantically is something the game itself will reveal.

Who are the voice actors?

Rockstar has not officially confirmed the voice cast for GTA 6. This is typical — they usually reveal voice actors after launch or very close to release.

Will there be more playable characters?

Not confirmed. GTA 5 had three. GTA 6 has confirmed two. Adding a third is possible but hasn't been announced.
