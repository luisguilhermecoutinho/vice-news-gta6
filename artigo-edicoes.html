Pre-Order Bonus — For Everyone

Regardless of edition, anyone who pre-orders GTA 6 before launch receives the Vintage Vice City Pack:

  • 🚗 Vapid Stanier '55 — classic '50s American sedan, available from the start of the story
  • 👕 '80s outfits for Jason and Lucia — Miami Vice-inspired clothing
  • 🔫 Iconic Vice City weapon pattern — exclusive pistol skin
⏳ Atenção: The Vintage Vice City Pack is a time-limited pre-order bonus — it disappears after launch. Pre-ordering now is the only way to guarantee it.

Ultimate Edition — Exclusive Weapons

Two exclusive weapon sets come with the Ultimate Edition:

  • Hawk & Little Morgan Revolvers — a "his-and-hers" pair with Vice City aesthetics. Jason has his, Lucia has hers. Custom engraved design.
  • Sidearms Personalizados — engraved versions of the pistol padrão de cada personagem: o Girardi ES9 para Jason e o Klose K17 para Lucia.

Ultimate Edition — Mod Shops and Extras

  • 🔧 Rideout Customs em Vice City — exclusive mod shop for Ultimate Edition owners, with paint options and upgrades not available in standard shops
  • 🔧 One-Eyed Willie's no Lake Leonida — second exclusive shop, specializing in off-road vehicles
  • 🚗 Classic Car Collection — exclusive side missions to hunt down 4 abandoned cars across the Leonida map. Each car has its own story and unique mods unavailable anywhere else
  • 🚣 Crest Kayak na safehouse de Jason — kayak available from the start, useful for discreetly exploring Kelly County waterways

Is the Ultimate Edition Worth It?

✅ Get the Ultimate Edition if...

• You love collecting vehicles and want the best from day one without unlocking them

• You value exclusive garages and premium mod shops

• You want the Classic Car Collection — unique missions not in the Standard edition

• You play a lot of GTA Online and want a vehicle head start from day one

• The $20 difference isn't significant for you

💜 The Standard Edition is fine if...

• You mainly play story mode and prefer discovering vehicles naturally

• Budget is tight — the base game is identical, no story content is cut

• You don't mind unlocking vehicles through normal gameplay

• You want to pre-order and see it first before spending more

💡 Nota importante: Neither edition cuts story content. The full GTA 6 campaign is available in both editions. The Ultimate Edition adds vehicle, weapon, and garage exclusives — not extra missions or story chapters.
