FNAC Leak: Three GTA 6 Editions Between €90 and €200 — What's Real?

21 jun 2026 · Por Vice News · Atualizado: 25 jun 2026 (após confirmação oficial)
✅ Atualização pós-confirmação (25 jun): A Rockstar confirmou apenas duas edições — Standard a $79,99 e Ultimate a $99,99. A edição "Collector's" de 200€ não se confirmou. Os preços europeus finais variam por retalhista.

On June 21, 2026, four days before the official pre-order announcement, the Portuguese FNAC inadvertently became the first European source to leak information about GTA 6's editions. SKUs identified as "RS1" to "RS5" appeared on the site with three distinct prices — and the Portuguese internet erupted.

What appeared on the FNAC website

89,99€
Standard (RS1/RS2)
119,99€
Premium/Deluxe (RS3)
199,99€
Collector's (RS4/RS5)

Was it real? Was it a placeholder?

At the time, a heavyweight insider in the GTA community poured cold water on it: the "RS1-RS5" codes are a generic SKU format that retailers frequently use as placeholders while awaiting official information. The prices could have been real, internal estimates, or simply working numbers.

What was right: the existence of multiple editions. What was wrong: the existence of a third physical "Collector's" edition at €200. Rockstar confirmed on June 25 only Standard and Ultimate.

What was confirmed for Europe

Official prices are in dollars: Standard at $79.99 and Ultimate at $99.99. In Europe, euro prices are set by each retailer based on exchange rates and local margins. FNAC, MediaMarkt, and similar retailers have been listing the Standard between €79.99 and €84.99 and the Ultimate between €99.99 and €109.99.

For those wanting the best deal in Europe: it's worth comparing prices between retailers — the difference can reach €10 between the most and least expensive, especially on pre-sale promotions.

Standard vs Ultimate — value breakdown

With confirmed prices, the question everyone is asking is simple: is it worth paying an extra €20 for the Ultimate? The answer depends heavily on your playstyle. The Ultimate Edition includes the Classic Car Collection — a set of restorable classic vehicles with their own story within the GTA 6 universe — 72-hour early access, and GTA Online credits for GTA 6. If you plan to play GTA Online seriously, the Ultimate pays for itself quickly.

Pre-order: is it worth the risk now?

With launch confirmed for November 19, 2026, pre-orders open, and final prices known, the remaining question is logistical: where to pre-order to guarantee day-one delivery? FNAC and MediaMarkt have historically been the most reliable retailers for day-1 launches. Other major retailers have been gaining ground with fast deliveries.

A word of caution: the physical edition requires you to be home to receive delivery or have in-store pickup available. For those who prefer not to depend on logistics, the digital version on PlayStation Store or Xbox Store is available from midnight on launch day — no queues, no waiting.
