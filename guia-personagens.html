GTA 6 Characters — Jason, Lucia & the Cast of Leonida

Updated: August 2026 · By Vice News · vicenewsgta6.com

GTA 6 introduces Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos as the two playable protagonists — a first for the series, which has never had a female lead in a mainline title. Here's everything confirmed about the cast of GTA 6.

Jason Duval

Jason is one of the two playable protagonists of GTA 6. He's a man navigating the criminal underworld of Leonida — loyal, resourceful, and deeply tied to Lucia. From what we've seen in the Extended Look, Jason has a rougher, more physical presence compared to Lucia's sharper instincts. He appears to operate from a safehouse in a rural area of Leonida before events pull him into Vice City.

Lucia Caminos

Lucia is the first female protagonist in mainline GTA history — a milestone the community has celebrated. She's sharp, driven, and the dynamic between her and Jason suggests a partnership that goes beyond crime. The Extended Look opens with Lucia in custody, which hints at a story that involves consequences from past actions. She's bilingual (Spanish/English), reflecting Vice City's diverse culture.

🌟 Historic moment: Lucia is not just the first female GTA protagonist — she's also one of the first Latina leads in a major open-world action game. The Dazed magazine cover shoot confirmed her as one of gaming's defining characters of 2026.

Supporting Cast

Based on leaks, official materials and voice actor portfolios, GTA 6 features a large supporting cast including characters tied to Vice City's criminal factions, law enforcement, media personalities, and the Keys underworld. A leak in June 2026 revealed at least one major secondary character whose name Rockstar has not officially confirmed.

Antagonists

GTA 6's antagonist lineup has not been officially revealed. The Extended Look hints at multiple power players in Leonida's underworld — from cartel-adjacent operations in the Keys to corrupt establishment figures in Vice City proper. Rockstar has kept this deliberately vague to preserve the story experience.

Jason & Lucia — Relationship Dynamics

The Extended Look made clear that Jason and Lucia's relationship is the emotional core of GTA 6. They're not just partners in crime — there's a complex dynamic between them that the Rockstar writing team has clearly invested in deeply. Fragments of dialogue suggest tension, trust, and loyalty tested by circumstance.

Lucia opens the Extended Look in police custody, being escorted — Jason is not present. This separation at the start suggests the story begins with the pair dealing with the fallout of a previous job. Their reunion (or plan to reunite) likely drives the early acts of the narrative.

The protagonist swap system allows players to experience key moments from both perspectives. Rockstar has not confirmed whether both protagonists are playable throughout the entire campaign or only at specific story beats — but the Extended Look suggests swapping is tied to the narrative, not an open free-roam feature like in GTA 5.

Criminal Profile System

A confirmed new feature in GTA 6 is the Criminal Profile system. As you play, NPCs, law enforcement, and the world react to your history of actions — your "reputation" affects how certain characters treat you, what missions become available, and potentially how law enforcement escalates against you. This is a deeper evolution of GTA 5's wanted system, bringing RPG-like consequences to your criminal career.

Voice Actors

Rockstar has not officially announced the voice actors for Jason and Lucia. The GTA series has a tradition of casting unknowns or semi-known actors who then become iconic in the community — Niko Bellic's Michael Hollick, and GTA 5's trio of Ned Luke, Steven Ogg, and Shawn Fonteno, none of whom were household names before GTA 5.

A June 2026 leak via a voice actor's portfolio page (since deleted) revealed a secondary character's name — suggesting GTA 6 has a large, professionally cast ensemble beyond the two leads.

Antagonists

Rockstar has not officially revealed GTA 6's antagonists. Based on the world-building visible in the Extended Look — Vice City's criminal factions, the Leonida underworld, and the scale of Jason and Lucia's operations — the game likely has multiple antagonist layers: a primary villain who represents the power structure Jason and Lucia are challenging, and secondary antagonists within the criminal factions they interact with.

GTA's best antagonists have always been mirrors of the protagonists — Dimitri to Niko's loyalty, Devin Weston to Michael's compromise. GTA 6's story structure, with two protagonists and a large cast, suggests a villain (or villains) who can credibly threaten both Jason and Lucia, individually and together.

NPCs and the Living World

Beyond the main cast, GTA 6 has 600,000 individual NPC animations — a number Rockstar confirmed — making it the most detailed crowd simulation in any open-world game. NPCs have daily routines, react to weather and events, and remember interactions. Recurring NPCs that appear across multiple areas of Leonida add texture to the world. This NPC depth is what makes Leonida feel like a living state rather than a backdrop.
