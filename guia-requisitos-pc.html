GTA 6 PC System Requirements — Can Your PC Run It?

Updated: September 2026 · By Vice News · vicenewsgta6.com

GTA 6 launches on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S on November 19, 2026. A PC version has not been officially announced, but a widely-reported leak points to February 2027 as the target date. When it does arrive, it will almost certainly be one of the most demanding PC games ever released.

Official Requirements

Rockstar has not published official PC requirements yet. They are expected to be announced in the weeks before the PC launch — likely January 2027 if the February 2027 leak is accurate.

📅 PC launch: Leaked date is February 2027, ~3 months after the console launch. Not officially confirmed. Read our full analysis: GTA 6 PC — everything we know →

Estimated Minimum Requirements

Based on the visual fidelity of the Extended Look and Rockstar's history of PC ports, here are our educated estimates for minimum requirements:

  • OS: Windows 11 64-bit
  • CPU: Intel Core i7-10700K / AMD Ryzen 7 5700X or equivalent
  • RAM: 16 GB
  • GPU: NVIDIA RTX 2080 / AMD RX 6700 XT (8GB VRAM)
  • Storage: 150+ GB SSD (NVMe strongly recommended)
  • DirectX: DirectX 12

Estimated Recommended Requirements

  • OS: Windows 11 64-bit
  • CPU: Intel Core i9-13900K / AMD Ryzen 9 7900X
  • RAM: 32 GB DDR5
  • GPU: NVIDIA RTX 4080 / AMD RX 7900 XTX (16GB VRAM)
  • Storage: 200+ GB NVMe SSD
  • DirectX: DirectX 12 Ultimate

Why These Estimates?

GTA 6 includes real-time weather simulation, dynamic NPC AI across massive distances, 700+ enterable buildings, ray-traced lighting across Vice City's neon skyline, and what appears to be the most detailed open world ever built. The PS5 and Xbox Series X are essentially mid-range PCs — meaning a comparable PC experience at 1080p/60fps is achievable on recent mid-range hardware, but 4K/60fps will require top-tier 2026-era GPUs.

These estimates will be updated when Rockstar publishes official specs.

GTA 5 PC Requirements — For Comparison

GTA 5 launched on PC in April 2015 with requirements that were considered demanding at the time. Here's what GTA 5 needed — and why GTA 6 will need significantly more:

  • GTA 5 Minimum: Intel Core i5 3470 / AMD FX-8350, 4GB RAM, NVIDIA GTX 660 2GB / AMD HD 7870 2GB, 72GB HDD
  • GTA 5 Recommended: Intel Core i7 3770 / AMD FX 9590, 8GB RAM, NVIDIA GTX 780 / AMD R9 290X, 72GB HDD

GTA 6 is built for PS5/Xbox Series X hardware — roughly equivalent to a high-end PC from 2021-2022. The baseline is far above GTA 5 in every dimension.

VRAM — The Critical Bottleneck

GTA 6's texture and asset quality will push VRAM requirements hard. At 1440p with high settings, 8GB VRAM will likely be the minimum — but 10-12GB is safer. At 4K with full detail, 16GB VRAM or more will be needed for stable performance.

Cards in a good position: RTX 3060 (12GB) for 1080p-1440p; RTX 4070 (12GB) for smooth 1440p; RTX 4080/4090 for 4K. On AMD, the RX 7800 XT (16GB) or RX 7900 XT (20GB) offer excellent VRAM headroom.

NVMe SSD — Likely Required, Not Optional

PS5 and Xbox Series X|S use ultra-fast SSDs as a core architectural requirement — GTA 6 streams its world based on that speed. On PC, a fast NVMe SSD will almost certainly be required, not just recommended. A traditional HDD will produce constant stuttering or fail minimum specs entirely.

If you currently run HDDs, budget for at least a 1TB NVMe SSD. GTA 6 will likely need 130-160GB of storage space.

Cloud Gaming — The Alternative

If your PC doesn't meet GTA 6's requirements, cloud gaming is a legitimate option. NVIDIA GeForce NOW and Xbox Cloud Gaming both let you stream demanding titles from remote servers. You'll need a stable connection (25Mbps minimum, 50Mbps+ ideal). For a single-player campaign, cloud gaming works well — just not for low-latency competitive play.
