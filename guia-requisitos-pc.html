GTA 6 PC System Requirements — Can Your PC Run It?

Updated: September 2026 · By Vice News · vicenewsgta6.com

GTA 6 launches on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S on November 19, 2026. A PC version has not been officially announced, but a widely-reported leak points to February 2027 as the target date. When it does arrive, it will almost certainly be one of the most demanding PC games ever released.

Official Requirements

Rockstar has not published official PC requirements yet. They are expected to be announced in the weeks before the PC launch — likely January 2027 if the February 2027 leak is accurate.

📅 PC launch: Leaked date is February 2027, ~3 months after the console launch. Not officially confirmed. Read our full analysis: GTA 6 PC — everything we know →

Estimated Minimum Requirements

Based on the visual fidelity of the Extended Look and Rockstar's history of PC ports, here are our educated estimates for minimum requirements:

  • OS: Windows 11 64-bit
  • CPU: Intel Core i7-10700K / AMD Ryzen 7 5700X or equivalent
  • RAM: 16 GB
  • GPU: NVIDIA RTX 2080 / AMD RX 6700 XT (8GB VRAM)
  • Storage: 150+ GB SSD (NVMe strongly recommended)
  • DirectX: DirectX 12

Estimated Recommended Requirements

  • OS: Windows 11 64-bit
  • CPU: Intel Core i9-13900K / AMD Ryzen 9 7900X
  • RAM: 32 GB DDR5
  • GPU: NVIDIA RTX 4080 / AMD RX 7900 XTX (16GB VRAM)
  • Storage: 200+ GB NVMe SSD
  • DirectX: DirectX 12 Ultimate

Why These Estimates?

GTA 6 includes real-time weather simulation, dynamic NPC AI across massive distances, 700+ enterable buildings, ray-traced lighting across Vice City's neon skyline, and what appears to be the most detailed open world ever built. The PS5 and Xbox Series X are essentially mid-range PCs — meaning a comparable PC experience at 1080p/60fps is achievable on recent mid-range hardware, but 4K/60fps will require top-tier 2026-era GPUs.

These estimates will be updated when Rockstar publishes official specs.
