Community · Map

Leonida's map rebuilt by the community — 6 regions, 700+ locations

Jun 27, 2026 · VICE NEWS / State of Leonida

While Rockstar hasn't released the official map yet, the community got there first. The State of Leonida project — built by the GTA mapping Discord — reconstructed the full map from the two official trailers, 63 screenshots and leaked footage.

The state is divided into 6 confirmed regions: Vice City (the neon heart), Leonida Keys (the southern cayos), Grassrivers (rural zone and rivers), Port Gellhorn (industrial port), Hamlet (suburbs), and Kelly County (open country).

On locations, Rockstar confirmed 700+ enterable spots: nightclubs, motels, restaurants, pawn shops, supermarkets, fast food, gun stores and Vice City's metro network. Unprecedented open-world density for the series.

The interactive map is live and updated with every new screenshot. If you haven't explored it yet, it's well worth your time.
