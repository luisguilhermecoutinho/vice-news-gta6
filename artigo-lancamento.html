The Date — Official Confirmations

GTA 6 was first announced with a release date in May 2025. A delay in November 2025 pushed the launch to November 19, 2026. Since then, the date has been reaffirmed multiple times:

  • Rockstar Games on the Newswire — official announcement with the November 19 date
  • Take-Two earnings call (August 2026) — Zelnick: "The date is firm. There is no delay."
  • Extended Look (Aug 27, 2026) — the date appears at the end of the video
  • Pre-orders open since June 25, 2026 — stores selling, including digital preload
💡 Nota: Take-Two projected $7.9–8.2 billion in net bookings for FY2027, "anchored on the November 19 launch." With that kind of public financial commitment, a delay would be catastrophic for the company.

What about PC?

Rockstar has not confirmed a PC release date. A leak points to February 2027, but nothing is official. Historically, GTA 5 came to PC 18 months after consoles. If the pattern holds, the wait could be long.

For PC-only players: the best option for now is to pre-order on console and sell later, or wait for the official PC version announcement.
