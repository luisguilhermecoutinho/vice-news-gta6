New GTA 6 Character Leaked Through a Voice Actor's Online Portfolio

22 jun 2026 · Por Vice News · Fonte: RockstarINTEL

It happened again — and this time the leak source was the most unlikely: a voice-over actor. On June 22, 2026, an actor whose name we won't reveal out of respect for their privacy updated their professional voice acting portfolio with a credit for a GTA 6 character that had not yet been officially announced.

The page stayed online for a few hours before being deleted — long enough for the RockstarINTEL community to take screenshots and archive everything. The internet never forgets.

What was revealed

The portfolio indicated the actor voiced a character with a name suggesting a secondary but significant role in GTA 6's story — possibly an antagonist or an ally of Jason and Lucia in one of Leonida's criminal factions. Out of respect for Rockstar's creative process and to avoid spoilers, we chose not to publish the name here.

⚠️ LEAK NÃO CONFIRMADO

Esta informação veio de uma fonte não oficial e foi removida rapidamente. A Rockstar não comentou. Tratamos este leak como não confirmado até haver confirmação oficial.

This isn't the first time

Leaks via actor and production staff portfolios are surprisingly common. In 2022, several pieces of GTA 6 information came from former Rockstar employees' LinkedIn profiles. In 2023, one of the biggest leaks in gaming history came from a direct hack of Rockstar's servers. Voice actors are instructed not to disclose credits for unannounced projects, but the rule isn't always followed.

What this means for the game

The existence of an expanded voice acting cast for secondary characters is good news: it means GTA 6 will have a story with depth and well-developed characters beyond Jason and Lucia. Rockstar has invested heavily in voice acting since GTA IV — and GTA 6 appears to continue that tradition.

Nota editorial: O Vice News optou por não publicar o nome específico da personagem ou do ator para proteger a privacidade do profissional envolvido e não comprometer a experiência de outros jogadores. Esta notícia serve para documentar o evento — o leak existiu e foi verificado pela comunidade — sem amplificar conteúdo que a Rockstar claramente não quis revelar ainda.

GTA 6's confirmed cast

What we know for certain is that Jason and Lucia are GTA 6's two playable protagonists — a duo Rockstar introduced in the inaugural trailer in December 2023. Lucia is the first female lead protagonist in the main series, and her relationship with Jason appears to be one of the narrative pillars of the game. From the dialogue fragments seen in the Extended Look, the dynamic between the two suggests a criminal partnership with emotional tensions that go beyond simple complicity.

Voice acting in GTA: a tradition of excellence

Since GTA IV with Niko Bellic, Rockstar has raised the bar for voice acting in open-world games. GTA V with Michael, Trevor, and Franklin set a benchmark that few games have matched — the three actors (Ned Luke, Steven Ogg, and Shawn Fonteno) did such iconic work that they became public figures in the GTA community. The investment in expanded voice acting for secondary characters in GTA 6 indicates Rockstar wants the same level of narrative depth — or greater.

The full GTA 6 story, with Jason and Lucia in Leonida, will only be fully revealed on November 19. But all signs are positive: a living world, characters with their own stories, and an expanded voice cast that guarantees rich dialogue in any open-world situation.
